# Speed, city roads against the navmesh, and the options: a review (2026-11-18)

Asked: look for compute gains without changing the addon's quality, compare the cities' navmesh data with the road data
(and look for collision data online), and go through the options' organization again. Everything is on the branch
`claude/perf-navmesh-options`, to validate in game.

## What changed on the branch

- **`agps navmesh compare`** (new dev tool, `app/azerothgps/navcompare.py`, nothing ships): every road the Router has in
  each city's Recast mesh, sampled every yard at its height, against the mesh; and the mesh's walkable ground no road
  comes near. Reports, renders and JSON in `data/debug/navcompare/`. Tests: `test_navcompare.py`.
- **Options > Map:** the six OpenMap stage sliders, the two inside-map sliders and their Reset button moved below the
  View section, under a new "Zoom levels" header (one new string, in all ten languages). Before, they filled the
  page's first screen and pushed "Turn the map with me" and the rest of View below the fold. No setting, default or
  page changed.

## Speed

Measured offline under the game harness (the whole addon, Lua 5.1), a three-stop route walked at 7 yd/s, 20 redraws a
second:

| part | per redraw |
|---|---|
| the whole redraw (G.Update) | 0.69 ms |
| the directions panel (G.UpdateNavPanel) | 0.16 ms |
| of it: Nav.Status / TeleportAheadText / StepsText | 0.05 / 0.05 / 0.03 ms |

The Lua work is small; the panel's text is already only set when it changes (`G.navPanelSig`). The in-game snapshots'
costs ("redraw: text" 5-14 ms/s, "arrow window" up to 20 ms/s at high frame rates) are the game's widget calls (SetText,
GetStringHeight, SetPoint, texture placing), which the harness's stand-in doesn't time. So no code change was made:
nothing measured here would show in game, and guessing at widget costs risks the quality. The way to find the next
gain is in game: `/agps debug perf` on a route through a city at 100+ fps, `/reload`, `agps perf`, then compare the
parts against the 2026-11-15 snapshot.

## City roads against the Recast meshes

`agps navmesh compare` (drops, lifts and swim roads left out: they're off the walked floors by nature):

| city | roads | yards | off the mesh | over no floor at all | runs of 3+ yd |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stormwind City | 1144 | 18741 | 312 (1.7%) | 61 | 27 |
| Thunder Bluff | 1117 | 14900 | 450 (3.0%) | 144 | 59 |
| Ironforge | 456 | 8513 | 528 (6.2%) | 127 | 48 |
| Undercity | 2902 | 32573 | 2537 (7.8%) | 45 | 283 |
| Darnassus | 2284 | 28934 | 2827 (9.8%) | 879 | 216 |
| Orgrimmar | 1504 | 18442 | 2634 (14.3%) | 775 | 228 |

The longest runs, to look at in game (x, y, height; the mesh's verdict):

- **Orgrimmar:** extracted roads 32-48 yd under the mesh's floor round (1512, -4788, 15), (1423, -4716, -2), (1442,
  -4744, 0), (1459, -4708, -5): about 400 yd in all. Either the road's heights are wrong there or the mesh lacks the
  ground under the city's model.
- **Ironforge:** roads 19-21 yd over the mesh's floor by the Great Forge, (-4842, -1112, 501) and (-4772, -1075, 503);
  roads over no floor at (-5074, -745) (the gate) and (-4621, -839) (the Mystic Ward).
- **Undercity:** roads at -45 with the nearest floor 22 yd over them at (1812, 423) and (1798, 349); a few with floors
  50-100 yd over (the Ruins above, so the road's own floor is missing from the mesh there).
- **Darnassus:** extracted roads over no floor at (10029, 1908), (9984, 1902), (10288, 2404), (10128, 1900); 5-6 yd off
  their floor by the Warrior's Terrace, (9679, 2504) and (9582, 2495).
- **Stormwind City:** 53 yd over no floor at (-9234, 184) (outside the gate); 7-12 yd under the floor along the canals
  by the Trade District, (-8706, 576), (-8727, 864), (-8686, 674).
- **Thunder Bluff:** a road over no floor at (-1518, 345), off the mesa's edge; the rest are short.

The "ground no road reaches" areas are mostly rooftops, mountain sides and the ground over the cities (Undercity's
largest are the Ruins up top): low value as they stand. A few are worth a look: Thunder Bluff's Spirit Rise round
(-956, 297, 135) by its mage, priest and shaman trainers, 44 yd from any road; the Hunter Rise round (-1421, -134).

**Read it as a second opinion.** The meshes are built from the same collision faces as the capital grids, so they can't
catch what the grids get wrong for the same reason. Drawn roads stay the truth: fix a road in game with the road tools,
not from this report alone.

## Collision data online

Nothing worth downloading. The public server meshes (vmaps and mmaps from TrinityCore, AzerothCore, CMaNGOS) and
pathfinding libraries such as namigator target the 1.12, 2.4.3 and 3.3.5 clients' geometry, so they don't match WoW
Forever's changed cities, and this project already builds its own meshes from the live client.

The one thing those pipelines have that ours lacks is **collision from objects the server spawns**: city gates, doors
and lifts (the `gameobject` table, their models from GameObjectDisplayInfo). AzerothCore keeps those as dynamic vmaps
and skips destructible models. The CMaNGOS and AzerothCore dumps are already in `data/thirdparty`, so adding those
objects' models to `recastmesh.py`'s triangles is the next step if a city's gate or lift shows up in the compare.

Sources: [TrinityCore extractor discussion](https://www.Github.com/TrinityCore/TrinityCore/discussions/26868),
[AzerothCore MapObject](https://www.azerothcore.org/doxygen/d2/d6f/namespaceMapObject.html),
[TrinityCore gameobject_extract](https://trinitycore.net/d4/dbe/gameobject__extract_8cpp_source),
[namigator](https://www.github.com/The-Alpha-Project/namigator),
[namigator crate](https://lib.rs/crates/namigator).

Done (no setting, default or page changed; each page's id kept):

- **Map:** the OpenMap stage sliders, the inside-map sliders and their Reset under "Zoom levels" after View, so the
  first screen is the world map, the style, the route color and the view.
- **Routing:** the four "Quest route: ..." options under their own "Quest Route" header (the button's name, already in

- **Routing:** "Ring the flight on the flight map" indented under "Use flight paths" (it was already gray with flights

- **Performance:** its last note sent players to "Map > Show on the map", a page gone since the map pages merged; it
  says "Map icons" now, in all ten languages.

Left for a decision:

- **Help** opens with "What's new in 1.2.0", three versions old. Bringing it up to date is new text in ten languages
  (and the old lines' translations dropped), so it's a release-notes choice, not a reshuffle.
- **Map and Map icons** are each more than two screens, mostly because the minimap's options live on them (the GPS
  map as the minimap: eight on Map; the game's minimap: four on Map icons). A Minimap page again (its id still opens
  Map, `O.PAGE_ALIASES`) would make both near one screen, but it reverses the 2026-11-10 decision to cut the map pages
  from four to three.
